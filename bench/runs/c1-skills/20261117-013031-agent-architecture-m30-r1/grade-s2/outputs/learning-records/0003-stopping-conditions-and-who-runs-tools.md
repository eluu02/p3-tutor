# Agent loop: solid on the structure, two gaps on stopping and who runs tools

On the repeat attempt of quiz 0001 (spaced practice), the learner correctly identified the loop as what makes an agent, that the model only outputs a tool request, and that the result is fed back as the next input. Two gaps remain: they believed an agent that keeps running will eventually fix a stuck problem (M6, no stopping condition needed), and their q4 reasoning said the model runs the tools itself (M4). They also said the model picks tools "by the name" only, not the full description.

**Evidence:** quiz 0001, submission s2, items q3, q4, q6.
**Implications:** Teach stopping conditions and budget limits soon, tied to the small model budget. Tool descriptions (name, purpose, parameters) are the natural next step toward the mission's tool-description goal.
